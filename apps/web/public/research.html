Cockpit

Research lab

The PAPER-only research lab: scenarios, backtests, suites, sensitivity.

Simulated paper research only.

Every step below runs on injected local data through the same deterministic code paths. There is no chain access, no wallet, no network, and no transaction. Outputs are bookkeeping over simulations — not live results, not advice, not profitability claims.

The pipeline

From a candidate plan to coverage — all offline and reproducible.

  1. 1
    strategy:plan

    Turn injected candidates into a PaperCandidate[] plan (manual hand-off).

  2. 2
    paper:run

    Simulate buys/sells against injected prices into an append-only journal.

  3. 3
    paper:backtest

    Replay one injected scenario into a deterministic, byte-stable report.

  4. 4
    paper:backtest:suite

    Run a directory of scenarios into a suite index.

  5. 5
    paper:backtest:sensitivity

    Perturb a base scenario and report per-variant deltas vs the baseline.

  6. 6
    paper:backtest:suite:coverage

    Report which simulated paper behaviours a suite exercised.
